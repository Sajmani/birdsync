## Missing, and needed

The community guidelines' rule on machine generated content links to a definition at
<https://www.inaturalist.org/pages/machine_generated_content>, which is **not vendored here**
and is blocked to automated retrieval like the rest. That page decides whether birdsync's
manner of posting falls inside a rule whose stated penalty is account suspension. See
[CR-012](../../decisions.md#cr-012--is-birdsync-machine-generated-content).

## What this is not

kept so a future reader can see what the terms said when the requirements were written.

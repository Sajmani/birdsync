Cornell Media Licensing Agreement Print

Modified on: Mon, 6 Jul, 2026 at 7:21 AM


When you first upload media to an eBird checklist using the Manage Media tool, you will be prompted to accept the license agreement below. The agreement must be accepted in order to contribute media using this tool. If you have any questions, please email us. Thank you for choosing to document your observations with rich media! Your participation is greatly appreciated. 


Note: The following agreement has been translated into additional languages for the convenience of our users. However, the English version represents the official version.
